ABOUT THE CONFERENCE

Research that connects intelligent systems

ICICPES 2027 is a proposed international forum for researchers, academics, engineers and industry professionals to share advances in communication systems, photonics, signal processing, intelligent embedded platforms and emerging electronic technologies.

Conference objectives

  • Present recent research and practical innovations.
  • Encourage collaboration across ECE-related disciplines.
  • Discuss challenges in reliable, secure and energy-efficient systems.
  • Connect emerging theory with real-world applications.

CALL FOR PAPERS

Key focus areas and technical tracks

Original, unpublished research contributions are invited in the following areas and related topics.

TRACK 01

Antenna and Wireless Communications

  • 5G/6G and next-generation networks
  • MIMO, beamforming and smart antennas
  • RF, microwave, millimetre-wave and THz systems
  • Wireless power transfer and energy harvesting
  • V2X and vehicular communications
TRACK 02

Photonics and Optical Communications

  • Optical wireless and visible light communication
  • Optical networks and photonic devices
  • Visible light positioning and optical sensing
  • Optoelectronics, lasers and photodetectors
  • Optical intelligent surfaces and photonic AI
TRACK 03

AI, Signal Processing and Data Analytics

  • Machine learning for communications
  • Deep learning and intelligent signal processing
  • Computer vision and edge AI
  • AI-assisted optimization and modelling
  • Sensor fusion and data-driven systems
TRACK 04

Embedded Systems, IoT and Robotics

  • AI-enabled embedded systems
  • Internet of Things and sensor networks
  • Real-time systems and edge computing
  • Robotics, automation and intelligent control
  • Cyber-physical systems and smart applications
TRACK 05

VLSI, Microelectronics and Hardware Design

  • Analog, digital and mixed-signal IC design
  • Low-power VLSI and hardware accelerators
  • FPGA-based systems and reconfigurable computing
  • Semiconductor devices and sensors
  • Hardware security and design verification
TRACK 06

Emerging ECE Applications

  • Biomedical electronics and wearable systems
  • Remote sensing and satellite communication
  • Smart grids and electronic instrumentation
  • Renewable-energy electronics
  • Quantum technologies and interdisciplinary systems

The technical tracks are a working draft and can be revised by the organizing committee.
